Meet the Owners

We know what it’s like to never be able to put your phone down.

Many service businesses grow through sheer determination, but that strength quickly becomes a liability as the operation scales. We know firsthand what happens when an owner cannot take a day off without the business grinding to a halt. Our Principal Consultant, Sirena, brings over 10 years of battle-tested operations management experience across the hospitality, food and beverage, and boutique wellness industries. She has stood exactly where you are—glued to a phone, answering the same questions from shift managers repeatedly because the answers either were not documented, or were documented in a bulky binder impossible to grab in the middle of a rush.

That firsthand understanding of operational burnout is why Chrysopoeia Frameworks was built. Equally owned by Sirena and Kai Marler, our partnership is rooted in the belief that durable growth requires honest evaluation. We believe trust is built by being direct, establishing clear expectations, and having the courage to acknowledge what actually needs to change on the floor.

We do not leave behind dusty PDF binders your team will abandon after the first week. By coupling familiar technology with our proprietary assessments, we build practical, actionable systems in the platforms you already use. We deliver a centralized, living operations HQ in a matter of weeks—without the months-long disruptions of traditional consulting. Our goal is to liberate you from daily bottlenecks, preserve your peace, and empower your team to take genuine pride in their work.
